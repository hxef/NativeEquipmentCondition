1.3.2
* The HUD CND bar and its word find a free spot by the ammo counter on any HUD layout and never leave the screen.
* The MCM sliders for the HUD CND bar count from the screen's top left and keep their numbers at any resolution.

1.3.0
* Worn guns are less accurate. Shots spread wider, the crosshair opens wider and the VATS hit chance drops. At 0 condition a gun keeps 75% of its accuracy.
* Worn guns lose range. Their damage starts dropping at a shorter distance, while up close nothing changes. At 0 condition a gun keeps 75% of its range.
* Wear widens the spread you would normally have, so perks, crouching and aiming down the sights help as much as before.
* Both work for NPCs too, and each has a slider on the MCM page. At 1, wear never lowers it.
* Item cards show a worn gun's Accuracy and Range, and the compare arrows and sorting follow them.
* The switches for slower fire and the VATS critical meter are gone, to make room on the MCM page. If you had one off, set its slider, Fire rate or Criticals at 0 condition, to 1.

1.2.1
* With a wear speed of 0, the gear you use stays in its stack.
* Clicking REPAIR while a trader's quantity box is open no longer opens the repair question over it.
* The message when a weapon breaks follows a mod, a script or the console that changes it during play.
* A game started with coc from the main menu reads the stats again too, so another DLL mod like RobCo Patcher no longer makes gear wear too fast or too slow there.
* On a pad, FallUI's LB button combinations at a trader no longer open the repair question.
* NEC.log no longer warns when another DLL mod, like Perk Conditions Framework, shares a part of the game's code with NEC without breaking anything.

1.2.0
* Works with UI replacers like FallUI and DEF_UI: CND on item cards, the HUD bar, quick container meters and the REPAIR button show where they belong. Anything NEC can't find is skipped and named in NEC.log or the bug report logs.
* The repair question is easy to read with FallUI Confirm Boxes.
* Quick container meters show with FallUI HUD and item sorters like FIS.
* The trader's REPAIR button no longer stays on screen over the INVEST box.
* Gear that gives nothing, like the Minuteman Hat, or rings and Dogmeat's bandana with AWKCR or similar mods, no longer wears, and worn ones go back to full.

1.1.6
* A workbench repair you can't afford shows the power armor station's list of what it takes, how many of each you have, and the ones you lack faded, instead of a line in the corner. That list and the repair's confirmation box both put the biggest count first.
* Every workbench repair, MEND included, plays the crafting sounds a mod build does. Workbench repairs change 5 fewer spots in the game's code, so fewer other DLL mods can switch them off.
* A workbench confirmation box with a long list of components stops above the bench's buttons instead of covering them.
* A trader's repair price counts NEC.ini's price multiplier as 10 at most, the MCM slider's top, so a bigger number typed in by hand no longer sells repairs for 1 cap.
* An alternate start mod that dresses you from the player's default outfit starts you in it at 100%.
* A weapon or piece of armor worn below 0.5% reads 1% wherever NEC shows condition, not 0%, since it still works. Only broken gear reads 0%.
* Another save or a new game, even one started with coc from the main menu, begins without the last game's jam, fire speed or HUD CND reading.
* A read only NEC.log no longer leaves a game start with no logs. NEC moves it aside as NEC.old.log, or a bug report log as NEC.trace.old.log and so on, and starts a new one. Every folder path NEC.log prints uses backslashes only.
* When another DLL mod takes over something NEC needs, NEC.log names the feature that stays off as a warning, not an error, and says when the only reason is that it can't read where that mod's hook leads. It warns when another DLL mod hooks the same spot both before and after NEC, which most likely crashes the game.
* The bug report log names damage types, like EnergyResist, on its lines for worn armor and weapons, where it left a blank.

1.1.4
* Workbench repairs and the rule that worn gear can't be modded work again beside Perk Conditions Framework, which skill mods like Classic NV Skill System use. The same goes for most other DLL mods that change the workbench menu.
* If another DLL mod takes over the spot where NEC says why an item at the workbench can't be modified, only NEC's "Condition must be full to modify." and "This item can't be modified." stop showing. Repairs keep working.
* A workbench repair you can't afford says the game's own "You lack the requirements to repair this item.", not "to create this item".
* The CND bars on the HUD and the worn energy damage on item cards keep working beside another DLL mod that changes them too and still lets NEC do its part.
* Gun Nut, Armorer, Blacksmith and Science! lower workbench repair costs even when a skill mod grants them, like Classic NV Skill System does from your Repair and Science skills.
* Another DLL mod that changes item stats as the game starts, like RobCo Patcher, no longer makes gear wear too fast or too slow, since NEC reads the stats again at every save load and new game.
* Fast automatic fire no longer stutters with a big inventory. Each shot, blow or hit taken used to update the Pip-Boy item card of every weapon and apparel item you carry.
* NEC.log and the bug report logs keep copies from your last 10 games, numbered like NEC.1.log to NEC.10.log, so a crash's logs are still there after you start the game again.
* NEC.log's "Checked 105 places again" warning now counts only the changes it explains, each with the DLL mod behind it.

1.1.3
* Fast automatic weapons wear less per shot, as if fired at an ordinary automatic's pace, so a Minigun lasts 3 times as long.
* Weapons that scrap into nothing, like Grognak's Axe, can be repaired at a workbench, costing and wearing as if made of what most weapons scrap into.
* A mod that doubles or halves every component's value no longer changes how fast weapons and armor wear.
* Gear at 100% always has its full damage, protection and fire rate. Some settings left it slightly lower.
* After a jam, the first reload in another save or a new game no longer skips its jam roll.
* Fixes a rare crash when repairing 1 of 2 identical items at a workbench or a trader.
* On the HUD, the CND bars show for a weapon that has not worn yet, and the quick container's item names keep their width when its condition meters are switched on mid game.
* NEC.log warns when NEC_custom.ini is saved as UTF-16, which NEC cannot read, logs an out of range setting with the value NEC really uses, and says when armor never wears.
* Beside DLL mods that change the same code, NEC keeps working past their small hooks instead of switching off and leaving worn armor at full protection. If one takes over the Pip-Boy's item cards, the CND row stays off instead of coming and going, and no name stays faded. NEC.log and the MCM page say exactly what was taken over, with no false or misleading lines.

1.1.1
* A new setting, Free mend above, sets the condition above which the workbench mends gear for free.
* With Workbench repair cost at 0, every repair is free and done on the spot.
* Legendary gear you find in chests or buy from traders arrives new. One NEC already rolled keeps its condition.
* Workbench repairs keep working alongside DLL mods that change the workbench item list, like Examine Lag Fix.
* A repair never asks for the components of an item's legendary effect, even where a mod like AWKCR gives legendary effects a recipe.

1.1.0
* Adds an optional Mod Configuration Menu page with every setting.
* A change on the page applies on the spot and is saved to NEC_custom.ini.
* The CND bars on the HUD can be moved, on the MCM page or in NEC_custom.ini.
* Works alongside other DLL mods that change the same things. NEC's MCM page and NEC.log name the mod and what is off.
* Changes typed into NEC_custom.ini by hand need a game restart, MCM changes do not.
