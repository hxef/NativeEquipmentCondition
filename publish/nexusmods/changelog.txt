2.0.0
* NEC.ini and NEC_custom.ini now live in Data\F4SE\Plugins\NEC\Config, so only NEC.dll and NEC.pdb stay loose in Data\F4SE\Plugins. NEC no longer reads a NEC_custom.ini beside the DLL, so move yours into Config by hand.
* A new MCM stepper, Pip-Boy repairs (iPipboyRepair, Off), lets you repair from the Pip-Boy as in Fallout 3 and New Vegas. Inspect a worn weapon or armor piece and press REPAIR. Spares only uses up a spare you carry, and Workbench opens the workbench's repair box with USE A SPARE, its levels paid in supplies and components from your inventory.
* A new MCM switch, Workbench repairs (bWorkbenchRepair, on), lets workbenches repair. When off, traders still repair, and the Pip-Boy does unless the Pip-Boy repairs MCM stepper is at Off. The Workbench modifications from MCM slider still decides which worn gear a workbench can modify.
* A new MCM dropdown, Preset (sPreset, Default), on NEC's main page picks Default, a preset NEC ships or one you downloaded or saved. Your changes to Default, a shipped or a downloaded preset show as (Custom) and stay apart from its file.
* 3 presets ship with NEC. Casual makes gear wear half as fast, repairs cost half and work in the Pip-Boy too, and loot turns up in better shape. Hardcore makes gear wear 1.5 times as fast, guns jam from 75%, loot turns up more worn and repairs cost more, and a repair that takes components asks the last rank of its crafting perk. New Vegas repairs from a spare in the Pip-Boy and none at the workbench, and weapons lose damage from 75% and armor protection from 50%.
* A grey line under the Preset MCM dropdown says what the picked preset does, and the new Describe preset MCM text field writes that line for a preset you saved.
* The new Save as new preset MCM text field saves your gameplay settings as a preset of your own to share, and the new Reset preset and Delete preset MCM buttons drop your changes to a preset or remove your own preset after you confirm.
* Presets go in Data\F4SE\Plugins\NEC\Config\MCM_Presets, where NEC never changes a file, and what you change in a preset or save in game goes in its Custom folder.
* Workbench repairs now take supplies (oil and cloth for guns and powered melee weapons such as the Ripper or a Heated Super Sledge, adhesive for other melee weapons, and adhesive and cloth for armor and clothing). From 87.5% a repair takes nothing else, and MEND, a spare and a trader take no supplies.
* The new MCM sliders Servicing supplies (iRepairSupplies, 1) and Supplies only from (fSuppliesOnlyFrom, 87.5, in steps of 0.5) set how many of each supply a repair takes and the condition from which it takes nothing else. Servicing supplies at 0 turns the supplies off.
* A workbench repair that takes components now needs the highest rank of Gun Nut, Science!, Blacksmith or Armorer that the item's mods ask for. Each rank you lack raises the condition the repair can start from, and pressing REPAIR then names the rank you need. The lock never holds back a trader or MEND.
* A new MCM stepper, Lock repairs (iRepairLock, By its mods), turns the lock off or asks every item for the last rank of its perk. A new MCM slider, Perk lock (iPerkLock, 100), sets the condition a repair can start from when the item asks for the perk's last rank and you hold none, and each rank short counts for an equal share of it, however many ranks the load order gives the perk.
* A new MCM switch, Lock spares too (bLockSpares, off), makes spares follow the repair lock, so a spare repairs only from the condition your crafting perk ranks allow.
* Each rank of Gun Nut, Science!, Blacksmith and Armorer says in its description from which condition repairs that need the perk can start. The perk chart and the Pip-Boy's perks page show a change on NEC's MCM page at once, with no save load.
* A new MCM slider, Perk component discount (iPerkComponentDiscount, 50), sets how much the last rank of a crafting perk takes off a workbench repair's components, each rank an equal share, and so how much more condition a spare restores.
* A weapon mod recipe that also asks for a combat perk, such as Rifleman on Nuka-World's Handmade Rifle barrels, no longer lets that perk set the repair discount.
* A new MCM stepper, Keep spare mods (iKeepSpareMods, Choose mods to use up), hands back the mods of a spare that a repair does not need as loose mods. Choose mods to use up and Choose mods to keep open a box where you pick them, and Cheapest used up first and Priciest used up first use up mods in that order until the repair is as good as the whole spare gives.
* A new MCM stepper, Spare mod rows (iSpareModRows, Change), sets whether a mod's row in the box that picks a spare's mods shows what the mod adds or costs, or the condition the repair reaches.
* Fixed the workbench not counting the components a spare left you until you reopened it, so a repair right after USE A SPARE can use them. The box that turns down a repair for lack of components now always shows which component is short.
* The Free mend above MCM slider is now Free mend from (fFreeMendFrom, 97.5, in steps of 0.5). MEND at a workbench now starts from 97.5%, where 1.9.2 mended from 96%, so gear from 96% to 97.4% takes a paid repair. Its NEC.ini key changed, so set the slider again if you had moved it.
* The Mod lock below MCM slider is now Workbench modifications from (iModdingFrom, 100), set in whole percent. Its NEC.ini key changed, so set the slider again if you had moved it.
* The MCM sliders Jams below, Weapon penalties below and Armor penalties below are now Jams start at, Weapon penalties start at and Armor penalties start at, each named by the condition where it starts.
* A new MCM switch, All NEC features (bNecOn, on), at the top of NEC's main page turns all of NEC off and back on while you play. While it is off, nothing wears, loot turns up new, NEC adds nothing to the HUD and menus, the presets and NEC's other MCM pages hide, and weapons and armor keep their condition.
* While the All NEC features MCM switch is off, food acts as vanilla food, but its age still counts, so food that got old in that time is spoiled once NEC is on again.
* A new MCM slider, Loose loot follows area value (fLootAreaEffect, 1), sets how much the condition of loot outside containers (on shelves, tables and the ground) follows how well stocked its area is. Food found there rolls its freshness the same way, and 0 turns it off.
* Gear made at a cooking or chemistry station, or at a mod's station of that kind, now comes out at 100% CND. It rolled a condition like loot before.
* Each weapon now wears against an ordinary weapon of its own damage type, so a mod that changes only ballistic damage no longer makes energy weapons wear slower. For vanilla's own weapons this moves wear by 4% or less.
* A new MCM slider, Suppressed weapon wear speed (fSuppressedWearRateMult, 1), sets from 0.5 to 2 how fast weapons with a suppressor wear as they fire, yours and NPCs'. A suppressor counts by the HasSilencer keyword, which every vanilla suppressor adds.
* New wear list ini files in Data\F4SE\Plugins\NEC\Wear give a weapon a wear speed multiplier (2 wears twice as fast) and the share of damage it keeps when worn, by weapon, weapon mod or keyword.
* Every place that shows condition, from the HUD and the pickup prompt to the quick container, the Pip-Boy apparel figure and the item cards, has its own MCM stepper to show it as a bar, as a percent or not at all. They replace the MCM switches Weapon CND bar, Power armor CND bar, Pickup CND bar and Loot preview meters, so a place you had switched off shows again until you set its stepper to Off.
* Renamed in MCM: the Weapon CND and Pickup CND rows of the UI page no longer say bar (Weapon CND from left), the power armor rows say Power armor dash CND, and the Vendor repairs header is now Trader repairs.
* Item cards show how fast an item wears or spoils beside its CND, against an ordinary item of its kind in your load order. A new MCM switch, Wear rate on item cards (bCardWearRate, on), turns it off.
* The CND row of an item card carries vanilla's + or - marks for the wear rate, as on Weight, with + when the item wears slower. In the Pip-Boy, in containers and at traders they compare with the item you have equipped, and at the workbench with the item as built while you try a mod. Under DEF_UI and FallUI the change shows as their Weight's does, + in red when the item wears faster.
* Item cards can draw CND as a framed bar in Fallout 4's look, like Fallout 76's cards, with the penalty mark where wear starts to cost. A new MCM slider, Item card CND bar thickness (fItemCardBarThickness, 1), sets how thick it is.
* A new MCM switch, Power armor CND on item cards (bPowerArmorCardCnd, off), shows a power armor piece's Health as CND on its item card. Power armor still wears and is repaired as vanilla does it.
* A new MCM stepper, CND decimals (iCndDecimals, Always), shows an item's condition with 1 decimal on its card, in the repair boxes and wherever a CND stepper shows a percent, and the MCM sliders that start from a condition count from that number. Fractions only leaves out a decimal of 0, and Off shows whole numbers.
* A new MCM switch, CND column in UI replacer lists (bCndColumn, on), adds a CND column to the Pip-Boy, trade, container and workbench lists of UI replacers whose lists have columns.
* Under FallUI, CND now stands at the same place on both item cards of its compare view.
* The Weapon CND percent stands under the CND word only where the ammo line runs between them, and reads in 1 line anywhere else, such as CND 87.4%. A new MCM switch, Weapon CND percent in 1 line (bHudBarPercentOneLine, off), draws it in 1 line at the ammo line too.
* The quick container now shows each row's CND at the row's right edge, and a new MCM switch, Quick container CND at the right edge (bQuickContainerCndAtRight, on), puts it back after the item's name when off.
* Fixed the quick container CND meters making the list report a huge size, which pushed the item card of More HUD Info to the screen edge at containers.
* The CND bars on the HUD and the Pip-Boy apparel figure now mark where penalties start with 2 small notches, as in New Vegas, once the MCM slider Weapon penalties start at or Armor penalties start at is from 0.01 to 0.99. No mark shows at 1, the default, or 0.
* A new MCM slider, Penalty mark width (fPenaltyMarkWidth, 1), sets how wide the penalty mark is, from 0.5 to 2. A new MCM switch, Penalty mark as a line (bPenaltyMarkAsLine, off), draws it as a plain faded line instead of 2 notches, and another, Pickup CND penalty mark (bPickupPenaltyMark, off), shows it on the Pickup CND bar too.
* The CND bars on the Pip-Boy apparel figure are now thicker, and a new MCM slider, Apparel figure CND bar thickness (fPipboyBarThickness, 1), sets their thickness from 0.5 to 2. A bar with no room above it stays thinner, so none covers the figure or a tab name.
* The Pickup CND bar now fills from the right, as the Weapon CND bar does.
* Each place that draws a CND bar has its own new MCM switch, such as Flip Weapon CND bar (bHudBarFlipped, off), that makes its bar fill from the other end, with the CND word on the other side where the bar has one.
* Under DEF_UI with an item sorter that puts a tag in front of item names, the Pickup CND now shows.
* The trader repair sign is redrawn in the style of the barter list's frame, and 2 new MCM sliders, Trader repair sign left or right (fTraderSignX, 0) and Trader repair sign up or down (fTraderSignY, 0), move it.
* The trader repair sign now takes short kind names when it shows as 1 line above the trader's list (as under UI replacers) and the full names do not fit. Kinds of the same level share 1 percent, such as Wpn/Arm/Clo 100%.
* At a trader, the repair box no longer says how far they repair while the sign on the barter screen shows it.
* At a trader, a repair price of 1 reads 1 cap, and the line for an item they can't repair any further says they repair only up to their level, where it said they can't repair past it.
* A workbench that can't repair an item sends you to a trader only while the Trader repairs MCM switch is on.
* The trader's repair lines now read correctly in Russian and Polish when a mod renames caps, and a word that differs from caps only in its capitals no longer counts as a rename.
* A line of a trader list ini file in Data\F4SE\Plugins\NEC\Traders can now set several kinds at once, split by commas, such as weapons 100, armor none.
* The trader list's lines in NEC.log and NEC.ui.trace.log name traders by ref ID, base ID and faction, as the list files do.
* The questions of the repair box at a workbench and at a trader, and of the box that lists your spares, are reworded, so they read right for an item with a plural name too.
* The Loot page in MCM no longer shows a header, since it was the page's only one.
* In German, Spanish, French, Italian, Polish, Brazilian Portuguese and Russian, the longest MCM helps are shorter, so MCM no longer shrinks their font.
* New Papyrus function SetFridge switches fridge cooling on and off for 1 container, whatever the fridge lists say, so a mod can make a fridge that cools only while it has power. The switch stays in the save, and the API text holds an example script. IsFridge answers for a switched container too.
* New Papyrus function GetItemRepairDiscount gives the perk discount of 1 reference, in the world or in an inventory, its mods included.
* New Papyrus function GetWeaponDamageShare gives the share of its damage 1 weapon keeps at a given condition, its mods and its wear list line included.
* New Papyrus functions GetFreeMendFrom, GetSuppliesOnlyFrom and GetModdingFrom give the conditions from which the workbench mends for free, repairs for supplies alone and lets you modify an item.
* The Papyrus functions GetFreeMendAbove and GetModLockBelow are gone, and so are the setting ids iFreeMendAbove:Balance and fModLockStart:Balance. Call GetFreeMendFrom and GetModdingFrom in their place, or read fFreeMendFrom:Balance with GetSettingFloat and iModdingFrom:Balance with GetSettingInt. For the same items, GetFreeMendFrom answers 1 more than GetFreeMendAbove did, except when it answers 0.
* The Papyrus API's functions that take a Form now take an item reference too and then answer for that reference, so GetRepairDiscount counts its mods. Given a reference, GetItems lists every stack of its base form.
* In the Papyrus API, GetRepairCost counts the supplies and answers None for a repair the lock holds back, GetRepairDiscount answers up to the Perk component discount MCM slider, and GetSpareGain answers -1 while the Lock spares too MCM switch holds the item back.
* In the Papyrus API, while NEC is off IsFeatureOn answers false for every feature, the write functions change nothing and answer false, and no new event comes. GetAccuracyShare, GetRangeShare and GetFireRateShare answer 1, GetRepairCost None and GetSpareGain -1, and the trader and scrap reads answer as with their feature off. GetSettingBool("bNecOn:Features") says whether NEC is on.
* In the Papyrus API, the argument afPercent of Damage, Repair and DamageAll is now afPoints, and akWeapon of GetFireRateShare is now akItem. Compiled scripts keep working, and only a script source that names these arguments needs the new names.
* README.txt and NEC_Papyrus_API.txt now ship in Data\F4SE\Plugins\NEC\Docs.

1.9.2
* On the barter screen, a sign under the trader's caps shows how far they repair weapons, armor and clothing, or says No repairs. The new MCM switch Trader repair sign (bTraderSign, on) turns it off.
* Under UI replacers such as FallUI - Inventory and DEF_UI, the sign shows above the trader's list.
* A trader whose stock is crowded with other goods now repairs that kind of gear a few steps lower, down to 30%, instead of not at all. The new MCM slider Leeway for other goods (iTraderLeeway, 8) softens that drop at higher values, and at 0 they do not repair it.
* A new MCM slider, Stock for a full repair (iTraderFullStock, 16), sets how many different items of a kind a trader must restock to repair it to 100%.
* New trader list ini files in Data\F4SE\Plugins\NEC\Traders set how far a trader repairs weapons, armor or clothing, named by ref ID, base ID or faction.
* Where a mod renames caps on the barter screen, the trader's repair box now uses its word, such as tickets in Fallout: London.
* A new MCM slider, Surprise loot 1 in (iLootSurpriseOdds, 100), sets how rare the looted item is that ignores its owner's usual condition, and 0 means no surprises.
* In the exclusion, food and fridge lists, a form ID that starts with FF is now skipped with a log line, since the game makes such forms for 1 save only.

1.9.1
* 4 new MCM sliders slow or stop the wear of your companions' and settlers' gear, on top of the NPC wear speeds: Companion weapon wear speed (fCompanionWearRateMult, 1) and Settler weapon wear speed (fSettlerWearRateMult, 1) on the Weapons page, Companion armor wear speed (fCompanionArmorWearRateMult, 1) and Settler armor wear speed (fSettlerArmorWearRateMult, 1) on the Armor page.
* Armor mods that change a piece's Health, by a multiplier or a flat amount, now make it wear slower or faster by the same share.
* A new MCM switch, Gains in the repair boxes (bRepairBoxGains, on), hides what each repair adds to the item's damage or resistances in the repair message boxes at a workbench and at a trader and in the message box that lists your spares.
* The MCM's Loot and repairs page is now 2 pages, Loot and Repairs.
* Papyrus API: a call on an item whose inventory the game has locked now waits until the lock is free and then answers, instead of answering as if the item no longer existed.

1.9.0
* A Papyrus API: functions to read and change the condition of any weapon, armor or food, the repair costs and the curves, and events when condition changes, an item breaks, a gun jams or food spoils. The API is documented in NEC_Papyrus_API.txt and in the Papyrus API article.
* NEC writes its logs in a folder of their own, Documents\My Games\Fallout4\F4SE\NEC, under the same names. The logs of earlier versions stay in the F4SE folder.
* NEC.papyrus.log logs the errors in other mods' calls to NEC, and with the Bug report logs MCM switch on, NEC.papyrus.trace.log lists the Papyrus API's calls and events.

A row too wide for its box takes short words such as +2 DMG, the Pip-Boy's word in each language, then a smaller font size, then a shortened item name.
* A new MCM switch, Repair rows in columns (bRepairBoxColumns, on), turns the columns off, so each row shows as 1 centered line as vanilla draws its boxes.
* When you repair with a spare at a workbench, the message box that lists your spares shows 1 mark for each spare, equipped when it is, else favorite, else legendary, and names the rank of the perk behind the item's repair discount, such as Gun Nut 2, when it raises the condition a spare restores.
* The perk chart draws a perk's whole description, so NEC's repair discount line on Gun Nut, Armorer, Science! and Blacksmith and the spare line on Scrapper are no longer cut off after 2 lines.
* The Jams below MCM slider no longer hides while you move the Jam chance at 0 condition MCM slider.

1.8.1

* The Jamming MCM switch is gone, and a jam chance of 0 turns jams off. If you had jams off, set the Jam chance at 0 condition MCM slider to 0 once.

* A new MCM stepper, Repair box opens on (iRepairBoxRow, Highest level), picks the row the workbench's repair box highlights as it opens: none, USE A SPARE, or the highest or lowest repair condition level.

* A pile of broken gear you dropped gives 1 component for each item when you scrap it in workshop mode, as scrapping them one by one does.

1.8.0

* A new MCM switch, Spare repair (bSpareRepair, on), turns USE A SPARE off, and a slider, Spare worth (fSpareWorthMult, 1), sets how much condition a spare restores, from 0.5, what scrapping it gives, up to 2.
* Each Scrapper rank raises what a spare restores, and the perk's description now says so.
* A new MCM stepper, Unscrappable spares (iUnscrappableSpares, Legendaries), picks which gear with the UnscrappableObject keyword can be a spare: none, legendaries only, or all.
* Worn weapons and armor scrap into fewer components at a workbench and in workshop mode, and even a broken one gives 1 component, its most common one.
* The Escape key now closes the repair boxes as CANCEL does, and on OG so does Tab.
* The MCM's Loot and repairs page has its own sections for workbench, spare and vendor repairs.
* The Spoiled food rads help, NEC.ini and NEC.food.log now say what the multiple adds: at 1, 15 rads for meat and 5 for other food on top of the food's own.
* The longest MCM helps are shorter, so MCM no longer shrinks their font.

1.4.6
* NPC weapons now wear as they fight, shot by shot and blow by blow, without jams. One worn out to 0 stays in their hands.
* 2 new MCM sliders set NPC wear apart from yours: NPC weapon wear speed (fNpcWearRateMult, 0.33) and NPC armor wear speed (fNpcArmorWearRateMult, 1). 0 stops that wear. Weapon wear speed and Armor wear speed now set only your own gear.

At 0.5, gear works as new down to half condition. Weapon jams start there too when it is under half.

* An essential NPC's own weapons never wear, like their armor, and leveled gear now counts as their own. Whatever you hand them does.
* srm armor also clears the condition an older NEC left on a ring, hat or other gear that gives nothing, worn or carried.
* When another DLL mod handles gun wear, NPC gun wear is left to it too.
* NEC.log now opens even when your Documents folder's name has letters outside your Windows language, like Cyrillic on an English Windows.

1.4.0
* Loading a save no longer rolls a condition for anything.
* Installed to a game in progress, NEC keeps your carried and stored gear at 100%, and so does loot spawned before you install or update NEC, or while Worn loot (bSpawnCondition) is off. Nothing to switch in MCM or the ini. * The switches for slower fire and the VATS critical meter are gone, to make room on the MCM page. If you had one off, set its slider, Fire rate or Criticals at 0 condition, to 1.
