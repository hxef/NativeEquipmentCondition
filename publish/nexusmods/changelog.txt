1.1.2
* Fast automatic weapons wear less per shot, as if fired at an ordinary automatic's pace, so a Minigun lasts 3 times as long.
* Weapons that scrap into nothing, like Grognak's Axe, can be repaired at a workbench, and cost and wear as if made of what most weapons scrap into.
* Weapons and armor wear as in vanilla under a mod that doubles or halves the value of every component.
* Gear at 100% always has its full damage, protection and fire rate, where some settings left it slightly lower.
* After a jam the next reload always works, and that no longer carries into another save or a new game.
* Fixes a rare crash when repairing 1 of 2 identical items at a workbench or a trader.
* On the HUD, the CND bars show for a weapon that has not worn yet, and the loot preview's names keep their width when its meters are switched on mid game.
* When another DLL mod takes over part of the Pip-Boy's item cards, NEC leaves the Pip-Boy's CND row off, instead of showing it only some of the time.
* NEC.log warns about a NEC_custom.ini saved as UTF-16, which NEC cannot read, logs a setting out of range as it is used, says when armor never wears, and no longer shows a false error or Pip-Boy count when another DLL mod changes the same code as NEC.

1.1.1
* A new setting, Free mend above, sets the condition above which the workbench mends gear for free.
* With Workbench repair cost at 0, every repair is free and done on the spot.
* Legendary gear you find in chests or buy from traders arrives new. One NEC already rolled keeps its condition.
* Workbench repairs keep working alongside DLL mods that change the workbench item list, like Examine Lag Fix.
* A repair never asks for the components of an item's legendary effect, even where a mod like AWKCR gives legendary effects a recipe.

1.1.0
* Adds an optional Mod Configuration Menu page with every setting.
* A change on the page applies on the spot and is saved to NEC_custom.ini.
* The CND bars on the HUD can be moved, on the MCM page or in NEC_custom.ini.
* Works alongside other DLL mods that change the same things. NEC's MCM page and NEC.log name the mod and what is off.
* Changes typed into NEC_custom.ini by hand need a game restart, MCM changes do not.
